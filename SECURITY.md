# Security Policy — AegisSovereign

**Maintained by Cristi Labs — Lead Architect: Marouan Rabai.**

- Report vulnerabilities privately to **security@cristilabs.net**.
- Please include: affected version (`AEGIS-SOVEREIGN-2026`), reproduction steps,
  impact assessment, and suggested mitigation.
- We acknowledge reports within **2 business days** and ship fixes by severity:
  Critical ≤ 7 days, High ≤ 30 days, Medium/Low ≤ 90 days.
- Do not run automated scanners against production systems without written scope.
- Red-team validation of this repo must stay inside `tests/`, `examples/` and
  local harness runs; the `examples/sovereign_bypass.py` simulator is for
  authorized quarterly T1–T7 exercises only.

We follow coordinated disclosure and credit reporters in `CHANGELOG.md`.
